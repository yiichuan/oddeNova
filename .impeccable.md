## Design Context

### Users

oddeNova serves creators who have musical ideas and aesthetic judgment but may not have formal theory, arranging, or electronic-music tooling experience. They use natural language to build music iteratively through listening, judging, and revising in a focused personal workspace.

### Brand Personality

Rational, internationalist, playful, and layered. The interface should feel systematically composed yet alive: clear enough to support focused creation, with moments of wit and responsive character that keep the tool from feeling sterile.

### Aesthetic Direction

Build the visual system on International Typographic Style principles: explicit grids, disciplined alignment, functional geometry, strong typographic hierarchy, and high legibility. Keep the primary composition flat and rational, then create spatial hierarchy through scale, spacing, contrast, overlap, light, shadow, translucency, and selective glass-like surfaces. Glass and illumination should make layers and interaction states tangible rather than become decoration. Introduce playfulness as a controlled break in the system through surprising proportions, icon behavior, asymmetry, or responsive details—not through visual clutter.

Motion should feel elastic, agile, modern, and game-like. Use responsive spring behavior, clear spatial trajectories, and immediate feedback to explain state changes and make controls feel physical. Game feel should come from responsiveness and consequence, not from indiscriminate bouncing. Every motion treatment must include a restrained `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative.

### Design Principles

1. Establish rational order with an internationalist grid, disciplined alignment, and typography-led hierarchy.
2. Build depth with deliberate planes: combine spacing, scale, overlap, contrast, light, shadow, and selective glass treatments.
3. Add wit at functional moments through controlled asymmetry, proportion, icon behavior, and interaction feedback.
4. Use elastic motion to communicate state, spatial relationships, and action results with modern game-like responsiveness.
5. Preserve speed, clarity, WCAG-readable contrast, and reduced-motion accessibility while keeping the creative workflow primary.
